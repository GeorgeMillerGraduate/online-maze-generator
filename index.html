How it works

Depth-first backtracking carves passages into unvisited cells. At a dead end, it returns to an earlier cell and tries another direction.

The result is a perfect maze: all cells are connected, with exactly one simple route between any two cells. Generate a maze, then choose Solve maze to reveal the route.

Change the speed while the animation runs. Clear cancels the current operation.
